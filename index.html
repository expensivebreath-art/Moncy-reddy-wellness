Cycle phases, simply explained

Menstrual

Bleeding days. Rest or move as comfortable; note flow and pain.

Follicular

From period start toward ovulation. Hormones and energy vary individually.

Ovulation

An egg is released. Calendar estimates can be off by several days.

Luteal

After ovulation until the next period. PMS symptoms may appear for some people.

When to consider medical advice

  • Bleeding lasts longer than 7 days, repeatedly soaks through a pad/tampon every hour for 2 hours, or causes dizziness or breathlessness.
  • Severe or worsening pain, fainting, fever, or unusual bleeding between periods.
  • Cycles repeatedly shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days, or a sudden persistent change from your usual pattern.
  • If pregnancy is possible and a period is late, consider a pregnancy test and seek medical advice if you are unsure.

Seek urgent care for very heavy bleeding with faintness, severe sudden pain, or possible pregnancy with pain/bleeding. This guide is general education, not medical diagnosis.
